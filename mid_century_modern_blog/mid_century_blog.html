Personal Reflection

I definitely do think I missed some things in it, but I understand the overall practice and what was to be done with this assignment. It was very challenging and I do feel myself constantly having to search for references and other things with how much information has now been presented for us to add but I do feel myself slowly getting the hang of it and remembering how to do certain things. But at the same time I do also feel myself rememebring more and more things and being able to do stuff by just thinking about it rather than having to find an outside source. I do feel that my css is a bit messy with the way that I do assignments because a lot of the time I struggle doing things in a specific order, so I constantly go from different section to different section, which sometimes I feel that I might be repeating things in my code. I defnitely did struggle a lot with trying to figure out font and the weight and other things, along with trying to align everything and I do think I still need some more practice with padding and margins. Honestly, I think I did everything to my best ability on my own, and would like to hear about what I did correctly and incorrectly before continuing changing things that I have so far. I also think I might want to have a review on alt text again because I am not fully confident on what I wrote for the images.
