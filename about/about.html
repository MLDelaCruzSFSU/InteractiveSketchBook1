But, who am I?

Hi, I’m Marvin Dela Cruz! I’m currently studying Visual Communication Design at San Francisco State University, with a focus on graphic and visual design. I’ve always enjoyed being creative, and design gives me a way to turn my ideas into something visual that other people can see and interact with.

As a student, I have enjoyed working on all kinds of projects ranging from posters to layouts of websites and different interfaces. I am most comfortable with Adobe Illustrator and InDesign, but I do have practice with other programs like Photoshop and Figma. I am always trying my best to learn new things and improve my skills.

Outside of school and work, I spend a lot of my free time on video games with friends, listening to music, and occasionally reading a book or two throughout the year. I enjoy all of the arts and I take a lot of inspiration from everything and try to incorperate the best things of what I enjoy into my designs.
